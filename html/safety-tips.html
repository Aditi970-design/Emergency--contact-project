Safety Tips & Emergency Guidance

Learn simple ways to prepare for unexpected situations.

Basic Safety Guidelines

Know Emergency Numbers

Keep emergency numbers accessible. Call 112 in India when immediate emergency assistance is needed.

Keep a First-Aid Kit

Keep basic first-aid supplies available and learn how to use them safely.

Share Your Location

When seeking help, clearly communicate your location and nearby landmarks.

Fire Safety

Leave a burning building using a safe exit. Avoid lifts and call emergency services from a safe place.

Road Safety

Wear a helmet or seat belt, follow traffic rules, and avoid using your phone while driving.

Personal Safety

Stay aware of your surroundings and seek help from trusted people or authorities when threatened.

Basic First-Aid Awareness

General guidance only. Follow emergency dispatcher instructions and seek professional medical help.

01

Severe bleeding

Call emergency services. Apply firm, continuous pressure with a clean cloth or dressing.

02

Unresponsive person

Check whether the person responds and is breathing normally. Call emergency services and follow the dispatcher's instructions.

03

Burns

Cool a thermal burn under cool running water. Do not apply ice, butter, or toothpaste.

04

Suspected fracture

Avoid unnecessary movement and seek medical assistance. Do not attempt to straighten the limb.

First-aid information is not a substitute for medical training. In a serious emergency, call 112 and follow the emergency operator's directions.
